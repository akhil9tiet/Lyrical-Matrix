Lyrical Matrix — Song Repetition Heatmap

Uncover the patterns and structures hidden within your songs.

Lyrical Matrix turns any song into a word-repetition heatmap. Search a track, and it fetches the lyrics automatically, then plots how often every word recurs and where it appears in the song — so you can see the chorus, repeated phrases, and structural DNA of your favorite music at a glance.

How Lyrical Matrix works

Analyzing a song takes three steps and a few seconds.

  1. 1. Search a song

    Enter the song title and, optionally, the artist name in the box above, or tap one of the example songs to see it in action instantly.

  2. 2. We fetch the lyrics

    Lyrical Matrix pulls the lyrics from the lyrics.ovh database and album artwork, release year, and a 30-second audio preview from the iTunes Search API.

  3. 3. Read the repetition heatmap

    The D3-powered matrix places every word once on each axis and colors a cell each time two positions in the song share the same word. Dense colored blocks reveal repeated words and phrases; sparse areas show one-of-a-kind lyrics.

What is a song repetition matrix?

A repetition matrix (also called a self-similarity matrix) is a square grid that shows how often a text repeats itself. In a lyrics version, the song's words run along both the top and the side. Every time two words match, the cell where they cross is filled in — so a chorus that repeats several times draws a dense, recognizable square in the matrix, while a verse of all-new words leaves gray space.

The technique became famous through Colin Morris's TEDx talk "Pop Music is Stuck on Repeat" and has been used to show how much of modern pop is built from repeating hooks and phrases. Lyrical Matrix brings that same structural analysis to any song you name.

Frequently asked questions

What is a lyrics repetition heatmap?

A lyrics repetition heatmap shows how often every word in a song recurs and where. Each word is placed once on the horizontal and once on the vertical axis, and a cell is colored in every time two positions in the song contain the same word. Dense colored blocks are repeated words, phrases, or choruses.

Which songs can I analyze?

Any song whose lyrics are available through the lyrics.ovh database. You search by song title and optional artist name, and Lyrical Matrix fetches the lyrics plus album artwork and a 30-second audio preview from the iTunes Search API.

Can I download or share the heatmap?

Yes. Every heatmap can be exported as a high-resolution poster image and shared directly to Instagram on mobile or copied to the clipboard on desktop using the Web Share API.

Do you store my songs or lyrics?

No. Lyrics and heatmaps are processed entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored on our servers, and you never need an account.

Is Lyrical Matrix free to use?

Yes. Lyrical Matrix is completely free, open source on GitHub, and there is no sign-up or download required.
